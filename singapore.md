## Views worth seeing

Most of the famous views are around Marina Bay, and the best ones are free and at night. A good first evening: Merlion Park at sunset, walk round the bay, Spectra at 20:00, then the Supertree show at 20:45.

## What to eat

Most of the best food in Singapore is at **hawker centres**: open-air food courts full of small stalls, where a meal costs about S$5–8. Michelin even lists some of the stalls. Don't skip them for restaurants.

### How hawker centres work

- **Find a seat first.** A packet of tissues on a table means it's reserved ("choped"). Don't sit there.
- **Order at each stall** and pay there. Many stalls take cash only; others take PayNow or cards.
- **Some stalls bring the food over.** Tell them your table number.
- **Return your tray** to the tray return points when you're done. It's expected, and you can be fined for leaving it.
- **Queue = good.** A long queue of locals is the best review there is.

### Ordering coffee and tea

| Say | You get |
| --- | --- |
| Kopi | Coffee with condensed milk (sweet) |
| Kopi O | Black coffee with sugar |
| Kopi C | Coffee with evaporated milk and sugar |
| Kopi O kosong | Black coffee, no sugar |
| Kopi siu dai / ga dai | Less sugar / more sugar |
| Kopi peng | Iced |
| Teh | The same words work for tea: teh, teh O, teh C, teh peng |

## Getting around

- **MRT and buses**: tap in and out with a contactless Visa or Mastercard (or your phone). No need to buy a travel card.
- **Grab** works for taxis and food delivery. Taxis from the stand are also fine and metered.
- **From the airport**: the MRT goes straight into the city, but a taxi or Grab is around 30 minutes and easier with luggage.

## Good to know

- **Weather**: hot and humid all year, around 25–32°C. Short heavy rain most afternoons, especially November to January. Carry an umbrella.
- **Indoors is cold.** Malls, the MRT and cinemas run strong air-con, so bring a light layer.
- **Rules are enforced.** No eating or drinking on the MRT, no durian on public transport, no chewing gum for sale, and no jaywalking.
- **Tap water** is safe to drink.
- **Tipping** isn't expected. Restaurants already add a 10% service charge plus GST.
- **Plugs** are the UK type (three square pins), 230V.
